| Metric | KalamDB SQL | KalamDB functions | TrailBase | PocketBase | SurrealDB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100k inserts | 3.18 → 3.16 s | 4.67 → 4.47 s | 5.39 → 5.26 s | 12.46 → 13.88 s | 2.41 → 2.71 s |
| 1M point reads | 13.90 → 14.93 s | 38.84 → 40.70 s | 12.85 → 13.15 s | 19.25 → 19.75 s | 13.98 → 14.46 s |

KalamDB SQL writes are flat. SQL point reads are slower on both passes
(15.16 s, then 14.70 s) than the 13.90 s October 25 run. Functions inserts
are a little faster on both passes. Functions reads sit between the
October 20 run (41.45 s) and the October 25 best (38.84 s).

Competitor binaries did not change, and their wall clocks moved too
(SurrealDB and PocketBase inserts especially). Treat shifts under a few
percent as run noise. The SQL read slowdown is larger than that noise and
showed up in both passes.

Ranking is unchanged: SurrealDB leads inserts, TrailBase leads reads,
KalamDB SQL is second on inserts and third on reads. On October 25, KalamDB
SQL reads were essentially tied with SurrealDB (13.90 s vs 13.98 s). Today
SurrealDB is ahead (14.46 s vs 14.93 s).
